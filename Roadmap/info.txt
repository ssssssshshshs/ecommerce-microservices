Interview Answer

If an interviewer asks:

Why use lb://PRODUCT-SERVICE instead of http://localhost:8082?

Answer:

lb://PRODUCT-SERVICE uses service discovery through Eureka. The API Gateway does not need to know the actual host or port of the service. Eureka provides available instances dynamically, and Spring Cloud LoadBalancer distributes requests among them. This removes hardcoded URLs and supports scaling and fault tolerance.

That's exactly why Step 12 exists in your project.
